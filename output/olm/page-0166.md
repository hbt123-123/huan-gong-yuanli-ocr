dQ = -q_{mc} c_{pe} dT_e \qquad dT_e = -\frac{dQ}{q_{mc} c_{pe}} \tag{4.4.11}

对(4.4.8)式微分,得

\[
d(\Delta T) = dT_b - dT_e
\] \tag{4.4.12}

将式(4.4.10)和式(4.4.11)代入式(4.4.12),得

\[
d(\Delta T) = \left( \frac{1}{q_{mc} c_{pe}} - \frac{1}{q_{mh} c_{ph}} \right) dQ
\]

或

\[
dQ = \frac{d(\Delta T)}{\frac{1}{q_{mc} c_{pe}} - \frac{1}{q_{mh} c_{ph}}}
\] \tag{4.4.13}

用 \( \Delta T_1 \) 和 \( \Delta T_2 \) 分别表示换热器两端两流体的温差,并对(4.4.13)式积分,得

\[
Q = \frac{\Delta T_2 - \Delta T_1}{\frac{1}{q_{mc} c_{pe}} - \frac{1}{q_{mh} c_{ph}}}
\]

或写成

\[
\frac{1}{q_{mc} c_{pe}} - \frac{1}{q_{mh} c_{ph}} = \frac{\Delta T_2 - \Delta T_1}{Q}
\] \tag{4.4.14}

联立式(4.4.9)和式(4.4.13),得

\[
K \Delta T dA = \frac{d(\Delta T)}{\frac{1}{q_{mc} c_{pe}} - \frac{1}{q_{mh} c_{ph}}}
\]

或

\[
\left( \frac{1}{q_{mc} c_{pe}} - \frac{1}{q_{mh} c_{ph}} \right) K dA = \frac{d(\Delta T)}{\Delta T}
\]

对上式积分,并将式(4.4.14)代入,得

\[
\frac{\Delta T_2 - \Delta T_1}{Q} KA = \ln \frac{\Delta T_2}{\Delta T_1}
\]

或

\[
Q = KA \frac{\Delta T_2 - \Delta T_1}{\ln \frac{\Delta T_2}{\Delta T_1}} = KA \Delta T_m
\]

其中

\[
\Delta T_m = \frac{\Delta T_2 - \Delta T_1}{\ln \frac{\Delta T_2}{\Delta T_1}}
\] \tag{4.4.15}
